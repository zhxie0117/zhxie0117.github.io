01 / ABOUT ME

Understanding the world.
Imagining what’s next.

I am an undergraduate student at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST) and currently an intern at ByteDance. I will join CUHK-Shenzhen as an incoming PhD student, advised by Prof. Li Jiang.

My research interests span visual tokenizers, video generation, unified multimodal understanding and generation, and embodied intelligence.

I have been fortunate to work with Prof. Xiang Bai and Prof. Yuliang Liu at the VLR Lab, and Prof. Wei Wei at the CCIIP Lab at HUST.
